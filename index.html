About

Hand gesture communication works by using a system that detects and interprets movements of the hands using sensors and algorithms. First, a camera or motion sensor captures the image or position of the hand. Then, the system processes this data using techniques from Computer Vision to identify the shape, position, and movement of the hand. After that, it applies Machine Learning models that have been trained to recognize specific gestures (like waving or pointing). These models compare the detected gesture with known patterns and decide what it means. Finally, the system converts this meaning into an action, such as moving a cursor, controlling a device, or translating gestures into words.
